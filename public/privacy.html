2. Мэдээллийг юунд ашигладаг вэ

  • Сургалтын түвшин, төлбөр, хуваарь, байршлын талаар хариулт өгөх
  • Сургалт сонирхсон хүнийг урьдчилсан бүртгэлд оруулж, ажилтан эргэн холбогдох
  • Ботын мэдэхгүй асуултыг сургалтын төвийн ажилтанд дамжуулж, хариуг нь танд буцааж хүргэх
  • Ярианы уялдааг хадгалах (өмнө юу ярьсныг санах)

Бид таны мэдээллийг зар сурталчилгаанд ашиглахгүй, зарахгүй, түрээслэхгүй.

Privacy Policy

Facebook Messenger chatbot of Sondor Korean Language Center
Last updated: 17 September 2026

This policy explains what information the Facebook Messenger chatbot of Sondor Korean Language Center collects, how it is used, and how it is protected.

1. Information we collect

  • Page-scoped user ID (PSID) — provided automatically by Facebook when you message the page. It is specific to this page and does not identify your Facebook profile.
  • Name — from your Facebook profile (first and last name) and from what you tell the bot when registering.
  • Phone number — only if you provide it yourself.
  • Messages you exchange with the bot.
  • Course preferences — level of interest, preferred start date and class time, as mentioned in conversation.

The bot does not access your friend list, photos, other profile content, location, or payment information.

2. How we use it

  • To answer questions about course levels, fees, schedule and location.
  • To register your interest so a staff member can contact you.
  • To forward questions the bot cannot answer to staff, and deliver the answer back to you.
  • To maintain conversation continuity.

We do not sell, rent, or use your information for advertising.

3. Third-party services

The bot relies on Meta/Facebook (messaging platform), Vercel (hosting), Upstash (database), OpenAI and Anthropic (AI models that generate replies), and Telegram (staff notifications). These process data solely to operate the service. Your messages are not used to train AI models.

4. Retention

  • Conversation history: deleted automatically 14 days after your last message.
  • Last-contact timestamp: 60 days.
  • Registration details (name, phone, level of interest): kept until you request deletion or they are no longer needed.
  • Unanswered questions: retained to improve the bot's knowledge base.

5. Data deletion

You may request deletion of your data at any time.

  • Message the Facebook page with "Delete my data", or
  • Call +976 90073136 or +976 88010931.

Within 7 days of the request we will permanently delete your conversation history, name, phone number and registration record, and confirm once done.

You can also revoke the bot's access from Facebook Settings → Apps and Websites.

6. Security

All traffic is encrypted over HTTPS. Every incoming Facebook request is signature-verified before processing. Access to stored data is limited to authorized staff of the center.

7. Children

The bot is not intended for children under 13. If we learn that we have inadvertently collected such information, we will delete it promptly.

8. Changes

Any changes to this policy will be published on this page with an updated date.
